FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Some details to help you prepare for our big day.

What is the dress code?

We'd love to see our family and friends dress up with us! We are requesting formal attire in our wedding colors.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to limited venue capacity, we are only able to accommodate the guests formally invited on your wedding invitation. If you received a plus one, it will appear when you search your name on the RSVP page.

Are kids allowed at the wedding?

While we love your little ones, our wedding is going to be an adults-only event so that everyone can relax and enjoy the evening.

Is there parking available at the venue?

Yes! There is free and ample parking available at both the church and the reception venue for all our guests.
